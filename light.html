Questions

Asked from the chair.

Will I look frozen or overdone?

A natural-looking result is the goal here. Our providers plan injectables conservatively, with you, so you look like a refreshed version of yourself rather than overdone. Outcomes vary from person to person, and your provider sets honest expectations at your consultation.

What does it cost?

Your initial consultation is complimentary, so you can get answers before committing to anything. Because the right plan depends on the treatment, your provider goes over any costs clearly with you, with no surprises. Call (480) 874-1515 with questions.

Who places the injections?

Tutera Medical is physician-founded. Facial injectables are placed by skilled, licensed providers at all three offices: Scottsdale, Chandler and Glendale.

Does it hurt, and is there downtime?

Most people find facial injectables quick and well tolerated, and many return to their day afterward. Comfort and any downtime depend on the treatment and the person, so your provider explains what to expect for your specific plan before you decide anything.

How long do results last?

It varies by treatment and from person to person. Your provider talks through a typical timeline for what you are considering, and what an upkeep plan might look like, during your consultation.

Is it right for me?

Aesthetic treatments are elective and not right for everyone. What is appropriate is decided at a consultation with a licensed provider, and a complimentary consultation is where that decision is made together. We will be honest about what will help, what won't, and when the answer is to wait or do less.
